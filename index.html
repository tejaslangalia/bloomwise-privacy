Bloomwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Bloomwise is a habit tracking app for Android, published by Tejas Langalia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Summary

Bloomwise does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in a local database on your phone so that it can work:

  • The habits you create, including names, icons, targets, schedules and reminder times
  • Your daily check-ins and the progress and streaks calculated from them
  • Your app settings, such as quiet hours, snooze duration and the first day of the week

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Backups and PDF summaries

You can export a backup file or a PDF summary of your habits. These files are created on your phone and saved only where you choose. If you share a file or save it to a cloud service, that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, vibration, run at startup and wake lock: used only to deliver the habit reminders you set, including after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: declared by components the app is built with. Bloomwise does not use them to send your habit data anywhere.

Access to files happens only through the Android file picker when you choose to save or restore a backup or save a PDF.

Third parties

Bloomwise contains no advertising networks, analytics services or social media integrations, and we do not share data with third parties. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and crash reports under its own privacy policy.

Children

Bloomwise is a general audience app and is not directed at children. Because the app does not collect personal data, we do not knowingly hold information about anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by deleting habits in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Exported backups and PDFs remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
